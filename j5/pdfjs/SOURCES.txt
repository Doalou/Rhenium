PDF.js 5.4.624 (legacy build)
=============================

Rhénium ships PDF.js, Mozilla's PDF viewer, as a separate built-in extension.
PDF.js is released under the Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE in this
directory, or https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Its image decoders
and colour profiles carry their own licences (web\wasm, web\iccs).

Rhénium embarque PDF.js, la liseuse PDF de Mozilla, comme extension intégrée
distincte, sous licence Apache 2.0.

Changes made by Rhénium (j5\fetch-pdfjs.ps1):
  - manifest.json, web\rhenium.js and web\rhenium.css added; viewer.html loads
    the last two;
  - source maps, debugger, sample PDF and scripting sandbox removed;
  - locales other than en-US and fr removed from web\locale.
